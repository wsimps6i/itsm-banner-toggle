# Privacy policy

ITSM Banner Toggle does not collect, send or sell any data.

- **No data leaves your browser.** The extension makes no network requests and has no
  server, analytics or tracking.
- **What it stores, and where.** Your settings (the hide switch, the new-announcements
  option and any custom selectors) are kept in your browser's extension storage, which your
  browser may sync to your own profile on other devices. To recognise banners you've already
  hidden, it keeps a short code worked out from each banner's text in local extension
  storage on this device (the most recent 300). The banner text itself isn't stored.
- **What it can see.** It only runs on the ServiceNow sites you add, each of which your
  browser asks you to allow. On those pages it looks for announcement banners and hides or
  shows them. It doesn't read or change anything else on the page.
- **Removing it** deletes everything it stored.

Questions or problems: [open an issue](https://github.com/wsimps6i/itsm-banner-toggle/issues).
